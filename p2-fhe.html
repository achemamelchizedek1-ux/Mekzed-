FHE Concepts

Homomorphic addition adds noise linearly — cheap and composable.

Homomorphic multiplication grows noise quadratically — bootstrap if noise > 80%.

Bootstrap refreshes the ciphertext, resetting noise so deep computation chains remain valid.
